A Journey to Visit Crafts and Time-Honored Shops

一生をかけてつくられたものが、ここにあります。

その一つひとつに、つくり手の人生そのものが込められています。

Here is something made with a lifetime of devotion.

A century-old craft. A sweet recipe passed down through generations.

Each one holds the maker's whole life within it.

Hyakunen Sanpo is a small invitation to come meet the real thing.
